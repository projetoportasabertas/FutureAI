About Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as pattern recognition, decision-making, and language translation.

Its impacts on the world are vast. On one hand, we have a massive increase in productivity and scientific breakthroughs. On the other, we face ethical challenges such as algorithmic bias, data privacy, and structural changes in the job market.
