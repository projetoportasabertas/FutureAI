How AI Works

What AI is

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, making decisions and learning from data.

Within AI there are different approaches, such as Machine Learning, in which systems learn from examples, and Deep Learning, which uses artificial neural networks inspired by the human brain to solve more complex problems.

In short, the process works like this: the algorithm receives a large amount of data, identifies patterns within that data, and starts performing the task on its own, without every step being manually programmed by a human.

Impacts on today's world

Artificial Intelligence already influences virtually every sector of society, bringing important benefits while also raising new questions that need to be carefully discussed.

  • Increased productivity: automation of repetitive tasks, freeing up time for more strategic activities.
  • Scientific breakthroughs: faster research in fields such as healthcare, physics and biology.
  • Ethical challenges: the need for responsible data use and transparent algorithms.
  • Shifts in the job market: new professions emerging and a growing need for reskilling.
  • Privacy concerns: greater care in protecting personal data collected by AI systems.
  • Environmental concerns: high energy consumption required to train large-scale AI models.
