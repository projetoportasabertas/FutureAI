Artificial Intelligence

Is a branch of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, making decisions and learning from data.

Within AI there are different approaches, such as Machine Learning, in which systems learn from examples, and Deep Learning, which uses artificial neural networks inspired by the human brain to solve more complex problems.

In short, the process works like this: the algorithm receives a large amount of data, identifies patterns within that data, and starts performing the task on its own, without every step being manually programmed by a human.

The Main AIs Today

The current market is led by several Artificial Intelligence tools, each with unique specialties, advantages, and limitations. Below, we highlight some of the most relevant ones:

  • ChatGPT (OpenAI): Stands out for its versatility in creating text, code, and general conversation. Pros: highly intuitive interface and a wide range of features. Cons: can generate "hallucinations" (incorrect information) with a tone of certainty.
  • Gemini (Google): Stands out for its native multimodal processing and integration with the Google ecosystem. Pros: fast access to real-time web information and fluid reading of images and videos. Cons: may have safety restrictions that block harmless prompts.
  • Claude (Anthropic): Stands out for its highly human-like writing and analysis of complex documents. Pros: has a massive context window, allowing it to read entire books or reports at once. Cons: lacks an integrated image generator in its main interface.
  • Copilot (Microsoft): Stands out as a productivity assistant geared towards work. Pros: direct integration with Windows and the Office suite, plus free access to OpenAI's latest models. Cons: the interface can be a bit cluttered and response times inconsistent.
  • Midjourney: Stands out in creating hyper-realistic and artistic images from text. Pros: aesthetic quality, lighting, and textures that are currently unmatched. Cons: operates through the Discord app, creating a learning curve for new users.
  • Llama (Meta): Stands out for being a family of open-source models. Pros: free for research and commercial use, allowing high customization by developers. Cons: requires technical knowledge and powerful hardware to run locally.
